# Branding

GPL-3.0-only grants rights to the software. It does not grant trademark rights to
the Bookmark Assistant name, logo or official service identity.

You may truthfully identify this project, acknowledge its origin and describe
compatibility. Do not imply that a modified distribution, hosted service or fork
is the official Bookmark Assistant product or is endorsed by its maintainer.
Use your own name and artwork for independently distributed products.

The official logo, favicons and marketing artwork are included in this repository
under GPL-3.0-only so that official releases have complete corresponding source.
That copyright license does not make them available as the identity of another
product. If you distribute a modified extension or operate your own service, replace
the name and logo in `packages/shared/brand.json`, `packages/shared/assets/brand`
and `packages/marketing/content`, and do not publish it under the Bookmark Assistant
name. Ask the maintainer before any other use as product identity. Existing licenses,
lawful uses and third-party trademark rights are unaffected.

These branding terms do not restrict modification, redistribution or commercial
use of the GPL code or artwork.

## Personal self-hosting and official services

Running a copy privately for yourself, including private modifications, does not
require rebranding or permission. A connection from the official extension does
not make an independently operated server an official service.

For a public fork or independently operated service, choose a distinct product
name, logo, favicon and promotional identity. Do not use official artwork or store
screenshots to suggest endorsement, and do not describe your service as the
official Bookmark Assistant service. Truthful attribution, compatibility statements
and lawful descriptive use remain permitted.
